Before you join

  • Use headphones if possible.
  • Test your microphone before speaking.
  • Keep guest contributions short so everyone gets a turn.
  • Be kind; respect privacy and chat boundaries.

Volunteer roles

  • Lead host / co-host
  • Chat moderator
  • Guest and schedule coordinator
  • Audio monitor / technical backup

Keep it safe & smooth

  • Have a backup host and contact channel.
  • Use only audio you have permission to broadcast.
  • Never share private details without consent.
  • If live audio fails, move to text updates and resume when safe.
