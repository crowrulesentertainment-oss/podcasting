Challenge Guidelines

Clear expectations for participating in the Thanksgiving and Christmas test challenges.

Who can participate?

Established podcasters, first-time creators, storytellers, audio and video makers, and people interested in learning. Creators from Tacoma, the Pacific Northwest, and beyond are welcome.

Does it cost anything?

No. Participation in the 2026 holiday test challenges is free.

Do I have to complete every prompt?

No. Participation is flexible. Choose the prompts and optional creator tests that fit your time and interests. Badge criteria will be published clearly.

Who owns my work?

You retain ownership of your original content. Submitting does not transfer copyright to CrowRules Entertainment. Public display or promotional use must follow the permission you provide.

Will my entry be public?

Not automatically. Public showcase entries require moderation and creator permission.

Can I include other people?

Yes, with their knowledge and permission. Do not publish private conversations or recordings of people who have not agreed.

Are paid opportunities guaranteed?

No. These are free community test challenges. They do not promise employment, compensation, sponsorship, or audience growth.

How do I submit?

Submission instructions will be published as the shared creator workflow is completed. This preview does not accept or store entries.

CrowRules may moderate or decline content that is unlawful, abusive, discriminatory, deceptive, or violates another person's rights.
