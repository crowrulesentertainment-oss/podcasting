HOW THE CHALLENGES WORK

Two holiday pilot events introduce a simple, welcoming way for independent creators to make something, share it with the community, and help CrowRules improve its future challenge experience.

Choose ThanksgivingChoose Christmas
The participation journey

Six simple steps

01

Choose a holiday event

Read the Thanksgiving or Christmas challenge page. Each has its own seasonal theme, dates, daily prompts and optional creator experiments.

02

Pick a prompt that fits you

Prompts are inspiration, not a requirement to complete every item. Choose a story, interview, reflection, or creator activity that fits your time and format.

03

Create an original contribution

Prepare an audio or video podcast contribution. Keep it respectful, original, and connected to the event theme. Get permission from anyone you record or feature.

04

Upload through Creator Studio

Sign in, open Creator Studio, select UPLOAD MEDIA, choose your show, add the episode title and description, select your media file, and complete the upload. Start the title with the event name so the team can identify it.

05

Challenge team verifies participation

During the pilot, CrowRules will manually identify and review qualifying entries. A Creator Studio upload alone does not automatically create a challenge record or grant a badge.

06

Receive recognition and choose public display

Once an eligible entry is approved, the creator qualifies for Founding Creator 2026. Public showcase placement and directory display require creator permission. Participation is not based on winning or popularity.

What you receive

Earned after one approved qualifying entry in either 2026 holiday pilot.

✦Double Event distinction

For approved qualifying entries in both Thanksgiving and Christmas.

Approved stories may be featured when creators grant permission for public display.

Pilot note: the current upload route uses Creator Studio. Separate challenge-entry records, automatic award issuance, public voting, and automated leaderboards are not yet connected. CrowRules will not publish unverified entries, vote totals, winners, or badges.
