01 / WHO WE ARE

Independent voices.
Connected possibilities.

CrowRules Podcasting is part of CrowRules Entertainment, an independent entertainment initiative based in Tacoma, Washington. We are building a creative network for podcast creators, listeners, community organizations and partners through original programming, storytelling and shared experiences.

Our approach is relationship-first: find common ground, agree on a useful collaboration and build something that serves both sides and the audience.

04 / HOW WE BEGIN

A straightforward
process.

  1. Introduce your organization. Tell us what you do and who you serve.
  2. Share the idea. Describe what a useful collaboration could look like.
  3. Discuss the details. Agree on scope, responsibilities, timing, costs and approvals.
  4. Confirm in writing. Launch only after both sides approve the plan.
05 / AUDIENCE & MEASUREMENT

Clear reporting.
Honest expectations.

We are developing our audience and partnership reporting systems. Audience size, reach and campaign results vary by platform and campaign. We do not publish invented audience figures or guarantee impressions, sales, leads or conversions.

Where available, agreed campaigns can include tagged links, referral traffic, content deliverables and post-campaign summaries. Measurement and reporting expectations are set before launch.
