A respectful, creative, and welcoming environment for listeners, creators, and guests.

These proposed guidelines describe the standards CrowRules intends to apply to public participation on CrowRules Podcasting, including profiles, podcast content, comments, groups, submissions, and live interactions where those features are available.

1. Treat people with respect

Do not threaten, target, harass, stalk, intimidate, or encourage violence against another person. Respect reasonable boundaries and do not use platform features to facilitate abuse.

2. No hateful or discriminatory abuse

Do not promote violence, dehumanization, or targeted abuse against people based on protected or personal characteristics. Context, reporting, and applicable law matter when reviewing content.

3. Protect privacy

Do not publish another person's private information, credentials, sensitive records, or identifying details without appropriate authority and consent. Do not use the Platform to dox or expose someone to harm.

4. Respect intellectual property

Only upload or share material you own or are authorized to use. Do not impersonate rights holders or submit knowingly false infringement claims.

5. Be authentic

Do not impersonate individuals or organizations, manipulate rankings or engagement, operate deceptive accounts, or misrepresent sponsorships and endorsements.

6. No spam or harmful activity

Do not flood the Platform, distribute malicious links or software, evade security controls, scrape restricted information, or interfere with service operation.

7. Safety of minors

Sexual exploitation of minors, grooming, solicitation, or attempts to obtain private access to minors are prohibited and may be escalated to appropriate authorities where required. Additional youth safeguards must be published before youth participation features are enabled.

8. Live and interactive spaces

Where live chat, groups, or real-time features are available, follow host and moderator instructions. Do not evade moderation or use live features to coordinate harassment or dangerous activity.

9. Reporting and enforcement

Users should be able to report concerns through an implemented reporting channel. CrowRules should review reports consistently, take proportionate action where appropriate, and provide an appeal path when available. Urgent threats may require immediate action.

10. Guidelines are not a substitute for law

Users remain responsible for complying with applicable law. These guidelines should be read with the final Terms of Service and other applicable policies.

Contact

Questions or safety concerns: crowrulesentertainment@gmail.com. For emergencies or immediate danger, contact local emergency services rather than relying on a platform report.
