Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of how personal information is handled on CrowRules Podcasting.

This draft describes the topics the final CrowRules Podcasting Privacy Policy must address. It must be reconciled with the live website, Supabase configuration, authentication providers, analytics, storage, email services, logs, and actual data-retention practices before publication.

1. Who is responsible

CrowRules Podcasting is a platform of CrowRules Entertainment. The final notice must identify the correct legal entity or individual responsible for the relevant processing and provide a verified privacy contact and mailing address where required.

2. Information that may be processed

Depending on features actually used, categories may include account identifiers and contact details; profile and creator information; podcast submissions and media files; listening, interaction, and preference data; support requests; reports and moderation records; device, browser, and security logs; and records of policy acceptance.

3. Sources and collection

Explain what information users provide directly, what is generated through platform use, what is received from connected authentication providers, and what is collected through cookies or similar technologies. Do not claim a category is collected unless verified.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Describe the specific purposes for account operation, authentication, hosting and playback, safety and moderation, customer support, security, legal compliance, and optional marketing. Where applicable, identify the legal basis for each purpose.

5. Service providers and disclosures

Identify relevant providers and explain the categories of information they process and why. The inventory should assess Supabase, authentication providers, hosting, email delivery, analytics, embedded media, and other integrations actually enabled. Do not imply that every provider receives all user data.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Explain essential session and security technologies, optional analytics or personalization technologies, user controls, and any consent mechanism required by applicable law. Link to the separate Cookie Policy.

7. Retention and deletion

Publish actual retention periods or meaningful criteria for account data, uploads, support records, reports, security logs, backups, and legal records. Explain what can be deleted, what may be retained, and why.

8. Privacy rights and choices

Describe applicable rights to access, correct, delete, obtain, or restrict use of personal information, as well as opt-out rights where applicable. Explain how to submit a request and how identity is verified proportionately.

9. Children and young people

State the Platform's actual age rules, safeguards, and handling of information relating to minors. Do not collect personal information from children in ways prohibited by applicable law. Obtain legal review of any service directed to or knowingly used by children.

10. Security and incidents

Describe security practices at an appropriate level without exposing sensitive operational details. Explain how legally required breach notifications are handled.

11. International processing

Identify whether information is processed outside the user's jurisdiction and explain applicable transfer safeguards where required.

12. Changes and contact

Explain how policy updates are posted and when users will receive direct notice. Privacy inquiries may be directed to crowrulesentertainment@gmail.com pending verification of a dedicated privacy contact.

Required pre-publication data inventory

  • Map every form, table, storage bucket, log, and authentication provider.
  • Identify analytics, cookies, embeds, and email tools in production.
  • Document retention, backup, deletion, and export behavior.
  • Determine applicable Washington and other state privacy obligations.
  • Review minors' data, vendor contracts, and security incident procedures.
