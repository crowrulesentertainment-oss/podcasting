Copyright & DMCA

Respecting creators' rights and providing a clear path for infringement concerns.

CrowRules supports creators while recognizing that music, recordings, artwork, writing, trademarks, and other works may be protected by copyright or other intellectual property laws.

1. Ownership and permission

Creators retain ownership of original material they own. Users must obtain appropriate licenses, releases, and permissions for third-party material they upload or distribute. Platform hosting does not grant a user ownership of another person's work.

2. Submitting a copyright notice

A formal notice should include the required identification of the copyrighted work, the allegedly infringing material and its location, the complainant's contact information, the required good-faith and accuracy statements, and a physical or electronic signature. The final process must follow the applicable statutory requirements.

3. Counter-notifications

Where applicable, the policy should explain the required counter-notification information, how it is submitted, and the statutory process and timelines for forwarding, restoration, or further legal action.

4. Review and action

Reports should be logged, reviewed, and handled under documented procedures. Any removal, restriction, or restoration must follow applicable law and the Platform's actual capabilities.

5. Repeat infringement

Any repeat-infringer policy must be documented, consistently administered, and reviewed by counsel. The final policy should explain how repeat cases are assessed and what consequences may apply.

6. Designated agent and formal notices

No designated DMCA agent or mailing address is represented as active in this draft. If CrowRules seeks the benefits of applicable DMCA safe-harbor provisions, counsel should assess eligibility and complete any required Copyright Office designation and registration before the policy identifies an agent.

7. Contact

General copyright questions may be sent to crowrulesentertainment@gmail.com. This general email is not represented as a registered DMCA agent or a substitute for a compliant notice channel.
