This draft is not a declaration that CrowRules currently uses every technology described below. A production cookie and storage scan is required before this policy is finalized.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored by a browser. Similar technologies may include local storage, session storage, pixels, SDKs, and embedded content that can store or access information on a device.

2. Categories

Strictly necessary technologies

These may support authentication, session continuity, security, load balancing, and essential platform functions.

Preferences

These may remember settings such as theme, display, or other user-selected preferences if those features are enabled.

Analytics and performance

These may measure traffic, diagnose errors, and understand platform performance. They should only be described as active after the deployed site and vendor configuration are verified.

Third-party content

Embedded players, video providers, or external integrations may use their own technologies under their own policies.

3. Your choices

Explain how users can manage optional technologies through the actual consent interface, browser settings, and provider controls. Essential technologies may be necessary for requested services to function. Do not offer a consent control unless it is implemented and honors the choice.

4. Retention

List the actual lifetimes or criteria for each cookie and similar technology in a maintained inventory. Session and persistent technologies should be distinguished.

5. Updates and contact

Review this policy when technologies or vendors change. Questions: crowrulesentertainment@gmail.com.

Required cookie audit

  • Scan the public site and authenticated pages.
  • Inventory first-party and third-party cookies and browser storage.
  • Record provider, purpose, duration, and category.
  • Implement consent controls where required before optional technologies run.
  • Re-test consent behavior after deployment.
