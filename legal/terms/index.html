These proposed Terms of Service (“Terms”) are intended to govern access to and use of CrowRules Podcasting (the “Platform”), operated by CrowRules Entertainment (“CrowRules,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). They are a drafting framework and must be completed and approved before they become effective.

1. Acceptance and eligibility

The final Terms should explain how users accept the agreement, minimum age and eligibility requirements, any parental or guardian permissions, and the process for updated terms. Access must not be conditioned on an acceptance flow that has not been implemented.

2. Accounts and security

Users should provide accurate account information, protect their credentials, promptly report suspected compromise, and use only accounts they are authorized to access. CrowRules should describe its actual account suspension, recovery, and authentication practices here.

3. Platform use and prohibited conduct

Users must comply with applicable law and the final Community Guidelines and Acceptable Use Policy. Prohibited conduct should be described clearly, including harassment, impersonation, unauthorized access, spam, infringement, manipulation, and abuse of platform features.

4. Creator content and ownership

Creators retain ownership of content they own. Uploading content does not, by itself, transfer ownership to CrowRules. The final Terms must define the limited license needed to host, process, display, distribute, and promote content through specifically described Platform features, and explain how that license ends when content is removed.

5. User permissions and third-party material

Users are responsible for having the rights, permissions, releases, and consents needed for content they submit. Third-party music, recordings, artwork, trademarks, and other materials remain subject to their owners' rights and applicable licenses.

6. Moderation and enforcement

The final Terms should explain when CrowRules may review, restrict, remove, or disable content or accounts; how users can report concerns; how appeals work; and any urgent safety or legal exceptions. Enforcement must follow published procedures and applicable law.

7. Availability and changes

The Platform may change as it develops. The final Terms should explain maintenance, feature changes, service interruptions, and how material changes to these Terms are communicated.

8. Monetization, payments, and promotions

No creator earnings, subscriptions, refunds, fees, or payment obligations should be implied unless the relevant feature is active and governed by separate, clearly disclosed terms. Advertising and sponsorship disclosures must comply with applicable law.

9. Disclaimers and liability

Any warranty disclaimers, liability limitations, indemnity provisions, and consumer-rights qualifications require careful legal drafting tailored to the actual services and applicable law. No provision should waive rights that cannot legally be waived.

10. Termination

The final Terms should describe voluntary account closure, suspension, termination, access to user content, retention required for legal or security purposes, and any available appeal or review process.

11. Governing law and disputes

Governing law, venue, dispute procedures, and any arbitration or class-action provisions must be selected and reviewed by qualified counsel. They are intentionally not specified in this draft.

12. Contact

For general questions about this draft, contact crowrulesentertainment@gmail.com. A formal legal-notice address and designated agent details must be verified before publication.

Items requiring completion

  • Confirm the legal operating entity and business address.
  • Confirm age eligibility and youth safeguards.
  • Document actual content licensing and removal behavior.
  • Confirm moderation, termination, and appeal workflows.
  • Have governing law, liability, and dispute provisions reviewed by counsel.
