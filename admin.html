TRACKING DEFINITIONS

• Plays: canonical play events when available; otherwise show/episode totals are labeled as fallback estimates.

• Listening hours: summed duration from readable listening-session records only.

• Completed listens: sessions explicitly marked completed, not inferred from play events.

• Unique listeners: distinct IDs in the selected listening source; anonymous activity may not be identifiable.
