Our Tacoma foundation

CrowRules is being built in Tacoma, Washington. We especially welcome connections from Tacoma, Lakewood, Pierce County and the wider Pacific Northwest. The network is open to appropriate collaborators from across the United States and around the world.

IMPORTANT EXPECTATIONS

Clear expectations.
Respectful partnerships.

There is no fee to apply or join the partnership network. Current volunteer and community collaboration opportunities are unpaid; future paid roles may be considered as CrowRules grows, but are not promised. Sponsorships and advertising are optional, separate arrangements. No application guarantees acceptance, booking, publication, compensation or a particular outcome. Responsibilities, timing, credit, permissions, expenses and any compensation should be agreed in writing before work begins.
