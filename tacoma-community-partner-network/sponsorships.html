GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

Before we talk.

Does joining the partner network cost anything?

No. Applying to join the partnership network and participating as a network member are free. Sponsorship is optional and separate.

Are sponsorship rates published?

There is no fixed public rate card on this page. Scope, availability, placement and contribution are discussed for each proposal and documented in writing.

Can a sponsor choose the editorial content?

Sponsor recognition may be agreed, but sponsorship does not buy editorial control, a favorable review or a guaranteed opinion.

Do you guarantee views, reach or sales?

No. We will not promise audience size, impressions, leads, conversions or sales unless a specific measurable commitment is separately documented and mutually agreed.

Can individuals or smaller businesses inquire?

Yes. The network welcomes individuals, independent professionals, small businesses and organizations. Sponsorship is considered separately based on fit and capacity.

What happens after I submit?

Your inquiry enters the existing private partnership application workflow for review. A submission is not an approval or a commitment to provide sponsorship.
