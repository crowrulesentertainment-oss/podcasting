PLEASE KNOW BEFORE YOU APPLY

Built on clarity.
Grounded in respect.

Membership is free

There is no fee to apply to or participate in the partnership network. Sponsorship and advertising are optional, separate arrangements.

Current roles are collaborative

CrowRules is an independent company being built from the ground up. Current volunteer and community contribution opportunities are unpaid. Future paid opportunities may be considered as the company grows, but are not guaranteed.

Applications are reviewed

Applying does not guarantee acceptance, a directory listing, a media feature, a match or a specific project.

Agree before you begin

Discuss scope, ownership, credit, costs, schedules and expectations before starting any collaboration. No one should assume a commitment has been made until it is agreed.
