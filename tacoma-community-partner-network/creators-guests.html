GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Do I need to live in Tacoma?

No. Tacoma and the Pacific Northwest are the network's roots, but creators, guests and collaborators from elsewhere are welcome to introduce themselves.

Do I need experience or a large audience?

No. Share what you can bring and any examples you have. A portfolio or established following is not required for an initial introduction.

Is there a fee to apply?

No. Applying and network participation are free. Any separate sponsorship or commercial arrangement is discussed independently.

Are roles paid?

Current opportunities are collaborative/volunteer in nature and compensation is not guaranteed. Future paid roles may be considered as CrowRules grows, but should never be assumed.

Will I automatically be booked?

No. Each submission is reviewed for fit, timing and production capacity. A submission does not guarantee a response, appearance, project placement or publication.

Can I pitch an idea for a whole show?

Yes. Choose the new show / special project pathway and describe the audience, subject, format and what kind of team or support you are looking for.
