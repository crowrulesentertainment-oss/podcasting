PARTNER RELATIONSHIP COMMUNICATIONS

Email Center & communication templates

Prepare consistent, personalized partnership emails. This release creates and copies ready-to-send drafts; it does not send mail from the browser or expose a Resend API key. Automated delivery should be connected through a secured server-side email function after sender-domain verification.

Email safety: verify recipient addresses, avoid including private application notes, use organization-approved contact details, and obtain consent before sending newsletters or promotional campaigns. Keep transactional updates separate from marketing subscriptions.

Supabase Row Level Security must authorize administrator access; this page does not grant permissions. Only publish partner profiles after review and explicit approval. Public contact fields should contain organization-approved contact details only.
