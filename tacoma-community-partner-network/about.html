WHO WE ARE

An independent media company.
A growing community vision.

CrowRules Entertainment is an independent entertainment and media company being built from the ground up in Tacoma, Washington. Its vision brings storytelling, original programming, creative collaboration and community connection together under one company.

CrowRules Podcasting is one branch of that larger vision. The Partnership Network gives individuals and organizations a clear way to connect with the podcasting division, introduce their work, share opportunities and explore projects together.

We are starting with the people, places and stories around Tacoma, Pierce County and the Pacific Northwest. Our doors are open to connections beyond the region, too. A local beginning does not have to mean a limited horizon.

WHAT GUIDES US

The principles behind
the partnership.

01

Community before reach

We want growth to strengthen the communities and relationships that helped make it possible.

02

Clarity over assumptions

People should understand what is being offered, what is being requested and what has—or has not—been agreed.

03

Respect for every contribution

Time, ideas, experience, creative work and resources deserve honest discussion and appropriate recognition.

04

Room to grow

We are building step by step. We value practical beginnings, learning and sustainable progress.

A NOTE ABOUT WHERE WE ARE TODAY

Honest beginnings.
Realistic expectations.

Free to join

There is no fee to apply to or participate in the Partnership Network. Optional sponsorship and advertising are separate commercial arrangements.

Collaborative opportunities

Current volunteer and community contribution opportunities are unpaid. Future paid opportunities may be considered as CrowRules grows, but are not promised or guaranteed.

Reviewed, not automatic

Applications and community submissions are reviewed. Applying does not guarantee acceptance, a public listing, a feature, a match or a project.

Agreements matter

Before work begins, participants should discuss responsibilities, timing, credit, ownership, expenses and any compensation in clear terms.
