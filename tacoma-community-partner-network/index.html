WHY WE'RE HERE

Good things happen
when we connect.

The CrowRules Tacoma Community Partner Network is a free, voluntary collaboration network created to make it easier for people and organizations to find one another, share what they do and explore ways to strengthen the community.

This is not a paid membership club, a sponsorship package or a fundraising requirement. There is no fee to apply, no donation requested and no financial contribution expected. Participation is based on shared interest, mutual respect and practical collaboration.

01 / CONNECT

Make local connections

Discover businesses, nonprofits, creators, community resources and people doing meaningful work across Tacoma and Pierce County.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you join.

Does it cost anything to become a partner?

No. There is no application fee, membership fee, required donation or financial contribution expected. The network is based on voluntary participation.

Is this a sponsorship or advertising package?

No. This is a community collaboration network, not a paid advertising or sponsorship package. Partners may choose to share information about one another, but no promotion is guaranteed or required.

Do I have to appear in a video or interview?

No. Interviews, video features, written spotlights and other media opportunities are optional. You can participate in the network without appearing on camera.

Does applying guarantee that my organization will be listed?

No. Applications are reviewed before publication. CrowRules may request more information or decline a listing that does not fit the network's purpose.

Can organizations outside Tacoma apply?

Yes. The initial focus is Tacoma, Pierce County and the South Sound, but nearby communities and organizations with a meaningful local connection are welcome to introduce themselves.

Can I change or end my participation?

Yes. Participation is voluntary. Contact the network to request a profile update, removal or a pause in involvement.

How is my application information used?

Your submission is used to review and respond to your inquiry. Contact details and private application messages are not displayed in the public directory.
