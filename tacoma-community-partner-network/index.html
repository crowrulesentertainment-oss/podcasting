START SMALL · BUILD TRUST

One conversation.
A practical first step.

You do not need to commit to an ongoing partnership to get to know us. A manageable pilot can help both sides see whether there's a good fit.

01

One guest conversation

We plan one focused conversation with a podcaster, creator, organization representative or community voice. Topic, length, format and release timing are agreed together.

02

One social share each way

If both sides want to, each shares one approved post about the conversation through an agreed channel. Copy, tags and timing are approved before anything is published.

03

Try it, then decide

We can use a 30-day window as a starting point for planning. After the pilot, both sides decide whether to continue, change the format or simply stay connected.

WHY WE'RE HERE

Good things happen
when we connect.

The CrowRules Entertainment Partnership Network is the shared relationship hub for the parent company and its current and future branches. It brings organizations and individuals together around storytelling, media, community projects, creative production and mutually agreed partnerships.

The network welcomes both local and nationwide relationships. Some opportunities may involve sponsorship or paid advertising agreements; others may be voluntary collaborations, community features or guest appearances. Every arrangement is discussed separately, with expectations agreed in writing before work begins.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you join.

Does it cost anything to become a partner?

No. Joining the network is free, with no required donation or financial contribution. Optional paid sponsorships or advertising are separate arrangements.

Is this a sponsorship or advertising package?

The network itself is free to join and is not a required advertising package. Organizations may separately inquire about optional paid sponsorships or advertising placements. Any paid arrangement requires its own written agreement; no purchase is required to join the network.

Do I have to appear in a video or interview?

No. Interviews, video features, written spotlights and other media opportunities are optional. You can participate in the network without appearing on camera.

Does applying guarantee that my organization will be listed?

No. Applications are reviewed before publication. CrowRules may request more information or decline a listing that does not fit the network's purpose.

Can organizations outside Tacoma apply?

Yes. Tacoma and the Pacific Northwest are our starting point, but the partnership network is open to appropriate organizations and individuals nationwide.

Can I change or end my participation?

Yes. Participation is voluntary. Contact the network to request a profile update, removal or a pause in involvement.

How is my application information used?

Your submission is used to review and respond to your inquiry. Contact details and private application messages are not displayed in the public directory.
