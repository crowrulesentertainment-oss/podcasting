HOW IT WORKS

CrowRules creator payouts

1. EarnEligible podcast revenue is recorded against your CrowRules creator ledger.
2. ConnectStripe Connect handles creator onboarding, identity checks and payout details.
3. ReceiveWhen eligible funds are available, CrowRules can initiate the configured creator transfer flow.
Current platform model: eligible creator payments use the configured CrowRules marketplace fee model. The payout page displays ledger data and readiness; sensitive payment operations remain server-side.
