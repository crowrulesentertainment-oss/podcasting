Automatic platform fee: 20%

For eligible member payments processed through CrowRules, the platform uses Stripe Connect destination charges: 20% is collected by CrowRules Entertainment as the application fee and the remaining 80% is routed to the onboarded creator. Payment, refund, and reconciliation events are recorded server-side.

CrowRules tracks eligible podcast earnings on your creator ledger. Stripe Connect handles your onboarding, identity verification, payout details and transfer to your connected account. Sensitive bank information stays with Stripe.

How CrowRules creator payouts work

Eligible podcast revenue is recorded against your CrowRules creator profile. When available earnings are requested for payout, CrowRules creates a payout record, then the payout transfer is sent to your individual Stripe Connect account. Stripe then handles the connected-account payout to the bank information you supplied during onboarding.

This page does not collect or display your bank-account credentials.
