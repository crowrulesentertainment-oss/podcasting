How creator payouts work

CrowRules tracks creator payout records separately from Stripe Connect. Stripe handles the connected payout account; CrowRules displays the creator-side payout ledger returned by the secure finance service.

During testing, payout records may be empty until the monetization and payout pipeline generates an eligible transaction.
