How CrowRules creator payouts work

Eligible podcast revenue is recorded against your CrowRules creator profile. When available earnings are requested for payout, CrowRules creates a payout record, then the payout transfer is sent to your individual Stripe Connect account. Stripe then handles the connected-account payout to the bank information you supplied during onboarding.

This page does not collect or display your bank-account credentials.
