VIDEO CREATION STUDIO

Build a 16:9 YouTube video from your own images, titles, scene cards, and optional background music. Preview it, export a WebM video or a thumbnail, then send the finished file to the private YouTube uploader.

01 · Project & scenes

Use audio you own or have permission to use. Music is mixed into the exported video in supported browsers.
Project JSON stores scene text and settings, not the image or music files. Re-select source files after importing. Rendering happens in this browser; keep the tab open until export finishes.

02 · Preview & export

Ready to preview. Add images and adjust each scene.
Upload finished video ↗
Your project stays in this browser until you export it. The renderer creates WebM; browser support varies. If rendering is unavailable, export the thumbnail and project JSON, then use a video editor.

03 · Camera & microphone recording

Record a real camera-and-microphone video directly in this browser. Use a secure connection (GitHub Pages is HTTPS) and allow camera/microphone access when prompted.

● RECORDING
Camera is off. Enable camera and microphone to begin.
Optional tab/system audio may not be available in every browser. Camera and microphone recording works independently.
A separate microphone audio file will be created alongside the video, for audio-only podcast episodes.
Recordings are created locally in your browser as WebM files. Keep this page open until recording is stopped and downloaded. Review the recording before uploading; the YouTube upload page remains private-by-default.
