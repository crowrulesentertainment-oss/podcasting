About the Platform

A place for independent voices

CrowRules Podcasting is being developed to bring creators, listeners, guests, and podcast communities together.

CrowRules Podcasting is a platform of CrowRules Entertainment, an independent entertainment company based in Tacoma, Washington. Its aim is to support podcast discovery, creator participation, audio and video programming, and community connection.

Our approach

We believe independent creators should have opportunities to share their work, develop audiences, collaborate, and connect with listeners. The platform is being built with creator participation, clear expectations, and responsible community practices in mind.

For creators

Creator tools and resources are intended to support podcast presentation, production, publishing, and collaboration. Feature availability may change as development continues.

For listeners

The platform is intended to make it easier to discover programs, follow shows, and participate in podcast communities where those features are available.

Our commitment

CrowRules aims to develop a platform that values creator ownership, transparent policies, privacy, accessibility, and respectful participation. These commitments should be reflected in implemented features and published policies, not treated as guarantees beyond the Platform's actual capabilities.
