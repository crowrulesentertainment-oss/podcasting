MORE THAN A LOGO ON A PAGE

Good partnerships
move everyone forward.

CrowRules Podcasting is looking for genuine, two-way relationships, not financial sponsors. A partner can share CrowRules Podcasting with customers, members, employees or followers. In return, we can introduce the partner’s business, organization, services or mission to our listeners through appropriate mentions, links, features and content.

There is no fee to become a community promotional partner, and we are not asking partners to donate, contribute money, purchase advertising or provide financial support. A partnership starts with a conversation about what each side can comfortably share.

A PARTNERSHIP THAT DOES NOT REQUIRE A BUDGET

Share your audience.
We’ll share ours.

This is a relationship built on visibility, knowledge and community—not a request for money. You can tell your customers, members or followers about CrowRules Podcasting through a newsletter, website, social post, in-store mention or personal introduction. We can return the favor by introducing your organization to our listeners through relevant mentions, links, partner spotlights and agreed features.

01 / VISIBILITYTwo-way promotion

Exchange website links, social posts, newsletter mentions, audience announcements and relevant recommendations. We’ll agree on what feels natural for each audience.

02 / STORIESVideo interviews & spotlights

Where schedules and production capacity allow, explore an on-camera conversation, business or nonprofit spotlight, guest appearance or recorded story about the work you do.

03 / GROWTHMentorship & shared knowledge

Connect people through practical advice, introductions, skill-sharing, creator guidance, workshops or conversations about building a business or community project.

Other possibilities include event and initiative promotion, community resource sharing, audience giveaways where appropriate, volunteer opportunities, creator referrals and collaborative campaigns. Every activity is optional and agreed in advance—there is no expectation to provide money, free products, services or equipment.

A CLEAR WAY TO GET STARTED

From first hello
to working together.

Every partnership begins with a conversation. We discuss what each side would like to share, choose realistic activities and confirm the details together. There is no financial contribution or purchase requirement.

01
Tell us about you

Tell us what you do, who you serve and how you might introduce CrowRules Podcasting to your customers or community.

02
We review the fit

We make sure the audiences and goals make sense, and that the proposed exchange is practical for both sides.

03
Shape the proposal

Agree on simple promotional actions, timing, links, interview or mentorship ideas, and who will handle each task. Any optional paid project would be separate and only happen if both sides request and approve it.

04
Confirm and launch

Both sides confirm the plan and permissions before any public announcement, promotion or interview.

You do not need a sponsorship budget to partner with CrowRules Podcasting. We are looking for organizations willing to help each other be discovered: you share our podcasting network with your customers, members or followers, and we share your organization with our listeners in ways that fit both audiences.

The standard community partnership described here is non-financial: mutual promotion and agreed collaboration, with no payment, donation, purchase or financial contribution expected from either side. Specific activities depend on availability and mutual agreement. Any separate paid work would be optional, discussed independently and approved in writing by both parties before it begins.
