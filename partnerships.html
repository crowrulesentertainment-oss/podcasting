A CLEAR WAY TO GET STARTED

From first hello
to working together.

Every partnership begins with a conversation. We review the idea, discuss fit and agree on expectations before anything is announced.

01
Tell us about you

Share your organization, goals and the kind of collaboration you have in mind.

02
We review the fit

We consider audience relevance, shared values, capacity and practical details.

03
Shape the proposal

Discuss deliverables, timelines, promotion, resources and any costs or compensation.

04
Confirm and launch

Both sides approve the details before the partnership or campaign goes public.
