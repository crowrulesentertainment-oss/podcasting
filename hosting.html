How it works

From your first show to podcast directories

  1. Create your show. Add your title, description, category, artwork, language, and creator details.
  2. Publish an eligible episode. Your show must be published and have at least one published free audio episode with a working public audio file before its feed is ready for directory submission.
  3. Enable your CrowRules RSS feed. Open Hosting & Distribution in Creator Studio and save the generated feed URL to your show.
  4. Submit your feed. Use the same RSS URL when submitting your show to Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube Music, Amazon Music, and other supported directories.
  5. Track your listings. Update each directory's submission status and save its public show URL in your Distribution Center.
A clear distinction

What “free” means

CrowRules does not charge creators for podcast hosting or for generating their RSS feed. Directory services have their own submission, review, eligibility, and account requirements. Those third-party platforms operate independently, and CrowRules cannot guarantee acceptance or placement.

Free hosting and RSS feed access do not mean every third-party service or optional paid feature is free. Any separate paid service will be identified separately before you choose to use it.

Before you submit

Get your show ready

  • Use clear, accurate show information and square show artwork.
  • Publish at least one free audio episode with a working audio file.
  • Make sure the contact email associated with your feed is accessible for verification requests.
  • Check whether your show is already listed before submitting a duplicate.
  • Review each directory's current rules and complete any ownership verification it requires.
