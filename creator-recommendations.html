Why each recommendation appeared

Every recommendation now separates six signals plus underlying source detail: Performance evidence describes measured outcomes; Creator preference describes direct feedback; Memory describes longer-term preference history and freshness; Causal status states the evidence limitation. These layers do not get merged into a single confidence score. Evidence provenance identifies the source and measurement basis separately.

How adaptation works

Outcome evidence can change evidence context and priority. Your feedback can change presentation order only. Useful and Not useful express preference; Already doing this identifies an existing behavior; Dismiss removes a recommendation from your active view. None of these responses increases or decreases outcome sample size or evidence confidence.
