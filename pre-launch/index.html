01 / THE IDEA

WHAT IS CROWRULES PODCASTING?

It’s a podcasting platform in development with two sides that belong together: an experience for people who love to listen, and tools for people who want to create. The goal is to make finding a show, returning to a favorite episode, connecting with creators, and building an audience feel like parts of one connected home.

01 · FOR LISTENERS

FIND YOUR NEXT FAVORITE

Discover podcasts and episodes, explore shows by creators, follow the voices you care about, and build a more personal listening experience.

02 · FOR CREATORS

MAKE YOUR SHOW YOURS

Work toward a home for your podcast, episodes, show identity, publishing workflow, and the audience you’re building — whether you’re just starting or already producing.

03 · FOR COMMUNITY

MORE THAN A PLAY BUTTON

Bring listeners and creators closer through follows, likes, conversations, discovery, rankings, and community features as they become available.

02 / THE EXPERIENCE

WHAT WE’RE WORKING TOWARD

The vision goes beyond hosting audio. CrowRules Podcasting is being developed around the full relationship between a show and its listeners.

♫

Podcast and episode discovery

Browse shows, find episodes to try, and discover more of the independent voices and topics that interest you.

♡

Following and engagement

Follow shows and creators, like content, and participate in community interactions where those features are supported.

▤

A personal listener space

A listener-focused area for your profile and listening activity, with features such as saved content and listening history developed over time.

◈

Creator tools and show pages

Dedicated creator and podcast spaces designed to help organize shows, episodes, publishing, and audience information.

◎

Live and community experiences

The longer-term vision includes ways to bring people together around live podcast sessions and shared conversations.

↗

Growth and future monetization

We’re exploring sustainable ways for creators to grow and potentially earn from their work. Specific programs, eligibility, and terms will be announced only when ready.
