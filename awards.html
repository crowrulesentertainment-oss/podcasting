More than a trophy

Celebrating the voices behind the microphone.

A CrowRules award should recognize more than popularity. It should celebrate thoughtful storytelling, entertaining discussions, creative risks, welcoming communities, production craft, and the courage to build something from the ground up.

The philosophy

Built from the ground up.

Independent creators make room for conversations that might otherwise never happen. The awards give those contributions a place to be recognized.

  • ✓ Recognize original ideas, storytelling, and creative growth.
  • ✓ Make space for new voices and independent productions.
  • ✓ Publish understandable rules and transparent results.
  • ✓ Celebrate creators and listeners as one community.

Trust matters

Designed for fair recognition.

Before official voting opens, CrowRules should publish clear rules, nomination criteria, privacy practices, and a verifiable method for determining winners.

Clear requirements

Define eligible periods, category requirements, submission limits, and eligibility conditions.

Verified voting

Use server-side validation, abuse prevention, rate limits, and duplicate-vote protection.

Published results

Announce verified winners and document corrections or review decisions.

Important: Official nominations, published nominees, and vote submissions use Supabase. A nominee must be approved by an awards administrator before appearing on the ballot. Votes are restricted to one per account per category.
