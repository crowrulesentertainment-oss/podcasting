Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

CrowRules Podcasting is a division of CrowRules Entertainment, an independent entertainment and media company based in Tacoma, Washington. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the CrowRules Podcasting website and newsletter.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect information you provide, such as an email address submitted through a newsletter form, subscription and confirmation status, and information you voluntarily submit through website or community features.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to operate and maintain the website, confirm newsletter subscriptions, send newsletters and service-related announcements you request, process unsubscribe requests, respond to inquiries, and protect the services from misuse.

3. Google Gmail API

CrowRules Podcasting uses the Google Gmail API with the gmail.send permission to send email from an authorized CrowRules Gmail account. This permission is used for newsletter subscription confirmations and other email communications initiated by our service. The integration is not configured to read, search, or retrieve the contents of the account's Gmail messages.

Google API data is used only to provide the email-sending functionality described above. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop or improve generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. We do not transfer Google user data except as necessary to provide the requested service, comply with applicable law, or protect against abuse or security issues. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Storage and Service Providers

Newsletter subscription records are stored using Supabase. Google provides the Gmail API email-delivery service. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their applicable terms and privacy practices. OAuth credentials used by our email integration are maintained as server-side secrets and are not intended to be exposed to website visitors.

5. Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help operate CrowRules Podcasting, or when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, or our services.

6. Retention, Unsubscribing, and Requests

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the services, maintain subscription preferences, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. You can unsubscribe using the link included in newsletter emails. To ask a privacy question or request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have provided, contact us at the email address below. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

7. Children's Privacy

Our newsletter is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13 without appropriate parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services change. The current version will be published on this page, with its effective date shown near the top.
