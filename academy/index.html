ACADEMY FAQ

Good questions.

Is the Academy free?

The Academy learning paths and beginner learning resources are free to access. You need a free CrowRules Podcasting account to save progress, submit scored quizzes, and receive certificates.

Can I learn without an account?

Yes. You can explore course descriptions, read lessons, and watch the public video resources. Sign in when you want progress synced to your account and access to quizzes and certificates.

How do I earn a certificate?

Complete all four lessons in a course, then pass its quiz with at least 80%. The certificate is then issued to your signed-in account.

Are certificates professional licenses or accreditations?

No. They are CrowRules Podcasting Academy records of course completion. They are not college credits, professional licenses, or third-party accreditations.

Can I return to a course later?

Yes. When signed in, your lesson progress is saved to your CrowRules account and is available when you return.
